      Clème.

      Inspiration

      Clème. was born from a desire to make time feel calm rather than urgent. Most clocks demand attention — Clème. simply exists alongside you. The design draws from analog clock faces, Nordic minimalism, and the quiet rhythm of ocean waves, distilled into a single soft-hued screen.

      Aesthetics

      Every choice is intentional — pastel sage green to ease eye strain, Poppins at weight 300 for a weightless feel, and layered SVG waves that drift slowly at the bottom like a tide. Colour palettes shift between pastel families so the mood can match the moment. Nothing harsh, nothing loud.

      Features

      • Live clock with 12 / 24h toggle
      • Pomodoro timer with custom duration
      • Stopwatch with lap timer
      • World clock — 75+ cities searchable
      • 4 font families · 5 colour palettes
      • Immersive fullscreen mode
